Carbon accounting · In development

Carbon accounting, built for your sub‑segment.

EnergE is a self-service platform for mid-sized companies to measure, track, and report their carbon footprint, and to plan reductions. The math isn't the hard part. Collecting the activity data is: utility bills, shipping logs, supplier reports, and ERP exports. That's what EnergE is designed around.

No spam. Just occasional updates on what we're building.

What we're building

01

Data collection, automated

Pull activity data from utility bills, shipping logs, supplier reports, and ERPs, instead of re-keying it into spreadsheets every month.

02

Scope 3, made tractable

Scope 1 and 2 are largely solved. Supply-chain emissions aren't. EnergE aims to structure the estimates and keep them traceable where primary data is thin.

03

Specific to your sub‑segment

Generalist tools miss industry detail, like the powered terminals along a telecom operator's cable runs, and fall back to manual, site-by-site entry.
